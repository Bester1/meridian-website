How it runs

What happens between the form and the truck rolling

Every load goes through the same five steps. Same desk, same rate-conf template, same PoD flow. The only thing that changes is the lane and the freight.

01

Load posted

Shipper fills the form on this page — or WhatsApps the desk. Lane, equipment, dates, commodity. No account needed.

02

Carriers bid

The desk broadcasts to the carrier pool matched on corridor + equipment. Carriers reply with their rate; desk shortlists by margin, equipment-fit, and history.

03

Rate-conf signed

Shipper sees the chosen carrier's quote + the desk's mark-up. Both sign the same rate-conf PDF before anything moves.

04

Truck rolls + tracked

Driver picks up, mid-trip status checks, ETA updates. Detention timer starts if a depot holds the truck past the appointment.

05

POD + invoice

Photo PoD captured at delivery, invoice issued same day. Carrier paid on agreed terms, shipper paid on theirs. Both sides rate each other.
