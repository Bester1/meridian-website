How a Meridian deal runs

Fuel cargo or road freight — the sequence is the same. Verify first, paper the deal, execute visibly.

Verify the counterparty

Fuel: IMO + AIS position + owner in the shipping registers. Freight: CIPC registration, insurance certificates, operating licence, references.

Paper the introduction

NCNDA before names. IMFPA so the commission chain is agreed by everyone up front. No handshake deals.

Put the fit on record

Fuel: product, quantity, spec, price basis, discharge port. Freight: lane, equipment, rate, pickup and delivery windows.

Execute visibly

Fuel: SGS at discharge, escrow/LC settlement. Freight: tracked transit, check calls, POD on file, detention auto-claimed.

Close clean

Every deal logged. Every rating earned. The next deal starts with a record, not a promise.
