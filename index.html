The conditions we work under

The same on both desks, published rather than negotiated. A condition you can read before you commit is worth more than one you discover afterwards.

Verification before introduction

A vessel by IMO number and live position, its owner confirmed in the shipping registers. A carrier by CIPC registration, insurance verified with the insurer, licence disc and permits. We will show you what we checked.

Paper before names

NCNDA signed before parties are introduced, IMFPA filed so the commission chain is agreed by everyone in writing at the start. No introduction does any work on a handshake.

Terms in writing before anything moves

What is moving, how much, at what price, from where to where, by when, and on what payment term. One document, held by both sides, agreed before commitment rather than reconstructed after it.

Execution you can see

Independent inspection and secured settlement on a cargo. Verified collection, position reporting and proof of delivery on a load. You are told where your goods are before you have to ask.

A record either side can call on

Every deal documented and kept. The second transaction with us starts from a file rather than from a promise, and so does any question about the first.
